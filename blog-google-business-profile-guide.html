How to Set Up and Optimise Your Google Business Profile (Step by Step)

PEKDigitals Team •16 November 2026 •4 min read

A Google Business Profile is the free listing that appears on Google Maps and on the right-hand side (or top, on phones) of Google search results when someone looks up your business. For many local businesses it brings more calls and visits than their website. Here is how to set it up properly, step by step.

What you will need: a Google account (ideally a business one, not your personal Gmail), your exact business name, address or service area, phone number, opening hours, a few good photos, and around 30 minutes.

Step 1: Create or claim your profile

  1. Go to google.com/business and sign in.
  2. Search for your business name. If it already appears (Google sometimes creates listings automatically, or a customer may have added it), choose it and request ownership. If not, choose to add a new business.
  3. Enter your business name exactly as it appears on your signage and documents. Do not add keywords or locations to the name.
  4. Choose your primary business category. Be specific: "Web designer" is better than "Business".

Step 2: Add your location or service area

There are two kinds of businesses on Google:

  • Customers visit you (a shop, salon, clinic, office): add your full street address so you appear on the map.
  • You visit customers (plumbers, cleaners, delivery-only businesses): you can hide your address and list the towns or areas you serve instead.

If you do both, add your address and your service areas.

Step 3: Add contact details

Add a phone number that is answered during business hours, and your website address. If you do not have a website yet, Google lets you link to a social page, but a proper website builds far more trust. See our website cost guide for options.

Step 4: Verify your business

Google needs to confirm that you really run the business before your profile goes fully live. Depending on your business type and location, Google may offer verification by phone or SMS, email, a short video recording showing your premises and signage, or other methods. Follow the instructions Google shows you; the options differ from business to business.

Tip: for video verification, film continuously: show the street or a landmark nearby, your signage, the inside of the business and proof of ownership such as tools of the trade or a business document. Many rejections happen because the video does not clearly connect the business to the location.

Step 5: Fill in every section

Complete profiles get more views and more trust. Work through:

  • Description: up to 750 characters on what you do, who you serve and what makes you different. Write naturally, mentioning your main services and location.
  • Hours: including special hours for public holidays.
  • Additional categories: any other categories that genuinely describe your services.
  • Services and products: list them with short descriptions and prices or starting prices.
  • Attributes: such as "Wheelchair accessible", "Online appointments" or "Accepts NFC payments", where they apply.
  • Opening date and social profile links.

Step 6: Add great photos

Businesses with photos tend to get more requests for directions and website clicks. Add:

  • Your logo and a cover photo.
  • Exterior photos that help people recognise your building from the street.
  • Interior photos, your team at work, and your products or finished projects.

Use real, well-lit photos. Add a few new ones every month to keep the profile fresh.

Step 7: Start collecting reviews

From your profile dashboard you can copy a short link that takes customers straight to the review form. Send it on WhatsApp after every completed job, print it as a QR code for your counter, and add it to receipts or invoices. Reply to every review, positive or negative, politely and promptly.

Follow Google's review content policies: never buy reviews, offer incentives for them, or write them for yourself.

Step 8: Keep the profile active

  • Post updates every week or two: offers, events, new products, useful tips.
  • Answer questions in the Q&A section, and add common questions yourself.
  • Turn on messaging if you can respond quickly.
  • Update hours before holidays, and photos when things change.
  • Check performance monthly: how people found you, calls, direction requests and website clicks.

Mistakes that get profiles suspended

Google's guidelines for representing your business are strict. The most common problems are:

  • Adding keywords or locations to the business name.
  • Using a virtual office or address where you do not actually operate.
  • Creating several profiles for the same business.
  • Listing a call-centre or premium-rate number instead of your real number.
  • Changing key details (name, address, category) frequently.
