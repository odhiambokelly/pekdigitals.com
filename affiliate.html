Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I earn as an affiliate?+

PekDigital pays up to 10% profit on each completed freelancer project you refer. The more projects you refer, the more you earn — with no cap.

When and how do I get paid?+

Commissions are calculated at month end and paid within the first week of the following month via M-Pesa or bank transfer.

Do I need technical skills?+

No. PekDigital's team delivers all services — you simply connect businesses with us through your unique affiliate link.

How do you track my referrals?+

Every approved affiliate receives a unique tracking link. All projects that come through it are automatically credited to you.
