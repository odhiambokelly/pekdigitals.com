Frequently asked questions

Important details about joining and earning through the programme.

Who is eligible to apply?

Freelancers, independent marketers, creators, consultants and other professionals with relevant networks or the ability to introduce potential business clients may apply. Applications are subject to review.

Is the commission always 10%?

No. The programme offers up to 10% of eligible net project profit. The applicable rate and calculation must be confirmed by Pekdigitals for the referral before work proceeds.

Is commission calculated on the full client payment?

No. It is calculated on eligible project profit, not automatically on the full invoice value. Any exclusions, costs, adjustments and the final calculation should be explained in the partner terms.

When will I receive commission?

Commission becomes eligible for payment after the referred project has been verified as qualifying, completed and paid for by the client. The payment schedule and minimum payout rules should be confirmed in your partner agreement.

Can I refer more than one client?

Yes. Approved partners may submit multiple referrals, subject to the referral tracking process and programme terms.

Does submitting the form mean I am approved?

No. Submission is an application only. Pekdigitals must review and approve your application and confirm the referral process and applicable terms.
