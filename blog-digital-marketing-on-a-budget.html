Digital Marketing on a Budget: 12 Practical Tips for Small Businesses

PEKDigitals Team •22 October 2026 •4 min read

When money is tight, marketing is often the first thing cut. But going quiet online usually costs more in lost customers than a little smart effort would. These twelve tactics are free or cheap, and they work for small businesses and start-ups across Kenya.

The free foundations

1. Claim your Google Business Profile

It is free, it puts you on Google Maps, and it is often the first thing people see when they search for your business or your type of service nearby. Fill in every field, add photos and keep your hours accurate. Our step-by-step Google Business Profile guide walks you through it.

2. Set up WhatsApp Business properly

The WhatsApp Business app is free and gives you a business profile, a product catalogue, labels, quick replies and away messages. Since many customers prefer to WhatsApp rather than call, this alone can noticeably increase enquiries.

3. Ask for reviews, every time

Reviews cost nothing and influence almost every buying decision. Send happy customers your direct Google review link on WhatsApp straight after you finish the job, while they are still pleased. Reply to every review to show you care.

4. Make your social profiles complete and consistent

Use the same name, logo, phone number and description everywhere. Add your location, opening hours and a link to your website or WhatsApp. Incomplete profiles look abandoned.

Content that costs time, not money

5. Answer customer questions publicly

Every question a customer asks is a post, a short video or a blog article waiting to happen. "How long does a website take?", "Do you deliver to Kisii?", "What's the difference between these two products?" Answering them once publicly saves you time and attracts people searching for the same thing.

6. Film short videos on your phone

You do not need a videographer. Good light (face a window), steady hands and a clear point are enough. Show a process, a transformation, a delivery or a quick tip. Short video is where most organic reach is right now, as we explain in our 2026 trends article.

7. Use free design tools

Canva's free plan has templates for posts, flyers, price lists and menus. Create a simple template with your colours and logo, then reuse it so everything looks consistent.

8. Repurpose everything

One customer story can become a Facebook post, an Instagram carousel, a WhatsApp Status, a short video and a paragraph on your website. Create once, share in five places.

Build an audience you own

9. Start a WhatsApp broadcast or email list

Ask customers if they would like to hear about offers and new stock, and add the ones who say yes to a broadcast list or email list. Message them once or twice a month with something genuinely useful. Always get permission first; Kenya's Data Protection Act requires it, and it keeps your messages welcome rather than annoying.

10. Partner with neighbouring businesses

A salon and a boutique, a hardware shop and a fundi, a printer and a school. Cross-promote each other on social media, share flyers at each other's counters, or create a joint offer. It costs nothing and doubles your reach to customers who already trust a local business.

Where a small budget goes furthest

11. A simple, fast website

Social media accounts can be restricted or hacked overnight; a website is yours. It does not need to be big. A clear one-to-five page site with your services, prices, location, reviews and a WhatsApp button is enough to make you look established and to appear in Google searches. See our website cost guide for realistic budgets, and our packages, which start from a few dollars a month.

12. Small, targeted ads

Once your profiles and website are in order, a small daily budget on Facebook and Instagram ads, targeted to people within a few kilometres of your business, can bring steady enquiries. Start small, run one clear offer, and stop anything that does not bring messages within a week or two.

What to avoid when money is tight

  • Buying followers or likes. They never become customers and can get accounts restricted.
  • Being on every platform. Pick two and do them well.
  • Boosting random posts. Ads work best with a clear goal and a clear offer.
  • Not tracking anything. Ask every customer how they found you.

A 30-day starter plan

  1. Week 1: Google Business Profile and WhatsApp Business fully set up.
  2. Week 2: Ask your last 20 happy customers for reviews. Take 30 good photos.
  3. Week 3: Post three times on your two main platforms. Film two short videos.
  4. Week 4: Start your broadcast list and approach one partner business.

Then keep going. Consistency is the cheapest marketing advantage there is. When you are ready to grow faster, our digital marketing services are designed for small business budgets, and a free consultation costs nothing.
