The Complete Guide to SEO Best Practices in 2026

PEKDigitals Team •12 November 2026 •5 min read

Search engine optimisation (SEO) sounds technical, but at its heart it is simple: make it easy for Google to understand your website, and make your pages the most useful answer to what people are searching for. This guide covers the practices that still matter in 2026, in plain language, with a checklist at the end.

In one sentence: pick the searches your customers actually make, create the best page on the internet for each one, make sure the page loads fast and works on a phone, and earn mentions from other trusted sites.

How Google decides what to show

Google sends automated programs (often called crawlers) to discover pages, stores them in a huge index, and then, every time someone searches, ranks the indexed pages by how relevant and trustworthy they look for that query. You cannot pay Google to rank higher in the normal results. You can only make your pages easier to find, easier to understand and more deserving.

Google publishes its own SEO Starter Guide, which is worth bookmarking. Everything below is consistent with it.

Step 1: Find the searches that matter

Start with your customers, not with tools. Write down the questions people ask you on the phone, on WhatsApp and at the counter. Those questions are your keywords.

  • Use Google's autocomplete. Type the start of a search, for example "website design for", and note the suggestions.
  • Check "People also ask" boxes in the results for more questions.
  • Look at Google Trends to compare phrases, such as "web design" vs "website design" in Kenya.
  • Add location words where it makes sense: "plumber in Homa Bay" is easier to win than "plumber", and the people searching it are closer to buying.

Group similar searches together and give each group one page. One page per topic is better than ten thin pages that compete with each other.

Step 2: Get the on-page basics right

Title tags

The title tag is the blue clickable headline in Google's results. Put the main keyword near the start, keep it under about 60 characters and make it something a human would want to click. For example: "Website Design in Kisumu | Fast, Mobile-Friendly Sites".

Meta descriptions

The short text under the title. It does not directly change rankings, but a clear, specific description gets more clicks. Mention who it is for and what they will get.

One H1, then clear subheadings

Each page should have a single main heading (H1) that says what the page is about, followed by H2 and H3 subheadings that break the content into scannable sections, just like this article.

Descriptive URLs and image alt text

Prefer /services/seo over /page?id=123. Describe images in their alt text ("plumber fixing a kitchen sink in Homa Bay") so search engines and screen-reader users understand them.

Internal links

Link related pages together with descriptive words. If you mention local search on your services page, link to your local SEO page. This helps visitors and helps Google understand which pages are important.

Step 3: Write content that genuinely helps

The single most reliable SEO strategy is to answer questions better than anyone else. In practice that means:

  • Answer the question early, then go deeper.
  • Include local details, real prices or ranges, examples and photos that a generic article would not have.
  • Keep it up to date. Revisit important pages every six months.
  • Show who is behind the content and why they are qualified. Google's guidance calls this experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trust.

A good example is a "how much does it cost" page. People search for prices constantly, and few businesses answer honestly. Our own guide to website costs in Kenya was written for exactly that reason.

Step 4: Keep the technical health strong

You do not need to be a developer, but you should check these regularly:

  • Mobile-friendly: Google ranks the mobile version of your site. Open it on a cheap Android phone and see how it feels.
  • Speed: test with PageSpeed Insights. Large images are the most common problem.
  • HTTPS: your address should start with https and show a padlock.
  • Sitemap and Search Console: submit your sitemap in Google Search Console (free). It shows which searches bring visitors and warns you about errors.
  • No broken links: links that go nowhere frustrate visitors and waste the crawler's time.

Step 5: Earn links and mentions

When other reputable websites link to you, Google takes it as a sign of trust. The key word is earn. Buying links or joining link schemes can get a site penalised.

Good ways to earn links as a small business:

  • Get listed in genuine local directories and industry associations.
  • Partner with suppliers or clients who can mention you on their sites.
  • Publish something genuinely useful, such as a price guide, a local survey or a free checklist, that others want to reference.
  • Sponsor or take part in community events that are covered online.

Step 6: Don't forget local SEO

If you serve customers in a specific area, local SEO is often where the quickest wins are. Start with a complete Google Business Profile and follow our local SEO strategies.

Common SEO mistakes to avoid

  • Stuffing the same keyword into every sentence. Write naturally.
  • Copying text from other websites or manufacturers.
  • Having several pages that target exactly the same search.
  • Expecting results in a week. SEO usually takes three to six months to show clear results, and then keeps paying off.
  • Ignoring Search Console warnings.

Your SEO checklist

  1. List 10–20 real customer questions and group them into topics.
  2. Give each topic one strong page with a clear title, H1 and meta description.
  3. Add internal links between related pages.
  4. Compress images and add descriptive alt text.
  5. Check mobile speed with PageSpeed Insights.
  6. Set up Google Search Console and submit your sitemap.
  7. Complete your Google Business Profile.
  8. Earn at least one new genuine link or mention each month.
  9. Review your top pages every six months.
