This Privacy Policy explains how BIGKEYS ENTERPRISES, trading as PEKDigitals ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses and protects your personal data when you visit pekdigitals.com, contact us or use our services. We process personal data in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is BIGKEYS ENTERPRISES, P.O. Box 20 Nyangweso, Homa Bay, Kenya. For any privacy question or request, email support@bigkeysenterprises.com or call +254 719 613 330.

2. Personal data we collect

Information you give us

  • Consultation and contact forms: name, email address, phone number, company name, the service you are interested in, budget and your message.
  • Support tickets: name, email, phone, company, the issue you describe and any files you attach.
  • Newsletter signup: your email address.
  • Affiliate applications: name, email, phone, country, skills, experience and any portfolio link or notes you provide.
  • Clients: billing and contact details, project content, and the account access you share with us so we can deliver your services.

Payment information

Payments are handled by our payment providers (for example M-Pesa, card processors, PayPal or your bank). We receive confirmation of the payment and limited details such as your name, the amount and a transaction reference. We do not receive or store full card numbers or M-Pesa PINs.

Information collected automatically

Like all websites, our hosting and content-delivery providers receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site securely. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics on this website.

3. How we use your data and our legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a confirmation email: to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to you.
  • To deliver, invoice and support the services you buy: performance of our contract with you.
  • To send our newsletter: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To assess affiliate applications and pay commissions: performance of the affiliate agreement.
  • To keep business and tax records, and prevent fraud and spam: legal obligations and our legitimate interests.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where needed to run our business, with service providers who must protect it:

  • Google (Google Workspace / Gmail, Google Sheets and Google Drive): to receive form submissions, store records and send emails.
  • Payment providers: to process and verify payments.
  • Domain registrars, hosting and email providers: to register and run the services you order.
  • GitHub (website hosting) and content-delivery networks that serve our website files and fonts.
  • Authorities: when required by Kenyan law or a lawful order.

5. International transfers

Some of our service providers (such as Google and GitHub) store data on servers outside Kenya. Where this happens we rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards to protect personal data, as required by the Data Protection Act, 2019.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become projects: up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Client, invoice and payment records: for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting purposes (generally at least 5 years).
  • Newsletter subscribers: until you unsubscribe.
  • Support attachments: up to 12 months after the ticket is closed.

7. Storage on your device

When you submit a form, our website saves a temporary copy in your browser's local storage so it is not lost if your connection drops. It is deleted automatically as soon as our server confirms it has been received, and it is never shared with anyone else. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • be informed of how your data is used;
  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have data deleted where it is no longer needed or was unlawfully processed;
  • object to processing, including direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent (for example by unsubscribing from the newsletter);
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To make a request, email support@bigkeysenterprises.com. We will respond within the time required by law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (www.odpc.go.ke).

9. Security

We protect personal data with access controls, secure (HTTPS) connections and by limiting access to people who need it. No system is completely secure, but we will notify you and the Data Protection Commissioner of any breach as required by law.

10. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its "last updated" date.
